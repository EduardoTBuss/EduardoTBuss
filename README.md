I build applied AI systems and I test them like a researcher: with baselines, reproducible
inputs, statistics and explicit failure cases. I am a 6th-semester Computer Engineering
student at the Federal University of Pelotas (UFPel), with work spanning LLM workflows,
deep learning and quantum-fuzzy inference. I am looking for AI and ML engineering roles
where experiments have to become reliable software.

## What I work on

| | |
|---|---|
| **Applied AI** | LLM-based systems that combine a deterministic parser, a local or API model and human review. [Order Forge](https://github.com/EduardoTBuss/order-forge) turns PDF purchase orders into validated EDIFACT; its benchmark showed that catalog reconciliation, not reading the PDF, was the bottleneck. Also agentic workflows, MCP and persistent context across agents. |
| **Machine learning** | Neural networks from a NumPy MNIST classifier (hand-written backpropagation, 97-98% test accuracy) to CNN architecture studies in PyTorch and TensorFlow, published at ERAMIA-RS 2025. |
| **Research** | At Tech&ApplieD-GM / Q-Flex (UFPel): fuzzy inference on quantum circuits, variational quantum classifiers against classical MLP baselines, FPGA emulation of quantum circuits in VHDL, and preregistered audits of context selection in LLM pipelines. |
